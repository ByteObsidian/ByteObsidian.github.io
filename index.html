Principles in practice

Understand first.
Test responsibly.

Good security work is careful, evidence-led, and clear about what to do next.

01

Establish scope

Work within explicit authorization and well-defined boundaries.

02

Investigate carefully

Use a methodical process and verify observations before drawing conclusions.

03

Communicate clearly

Make findings understandable, actionable, and oriented toward reducing risk.
